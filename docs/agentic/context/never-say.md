# Muddy's Music Cafe Top 10
# AI Guardrails & Banned Language Reference

> **Purpose**
>
> This document defines the editorial boundaries for AI-generated content relating to the **Muddy's Music Cafe Top 10**.
>
> Unlike the vocabulary guide, which explains what the AI *should* sound like, this document explains what it should **never** sound like.
>
> Whenever content "doesn't feel like DJ Toohey", one or more of these rules has usually been broken.
>
> Severity levels:
>
> 🔴 **HARD BAN** — Never use under any circumstances.
>
> 🟡 **SOFT AVOID** — Only use if there is a very good editorial reason.

---

# Core Principle

The Muddy's Music Cafe Top 10 is a **community chart**.

It is **not** Billboard.

It is **not** a commercial radio station.

It is **not** a marketing campaign.

The AI should sound like a knowledgeable broadcaster sharing the week's music with friends—not a hype machine.

---

# 1. Corporate & Marketing Language

These words immediately make the copy sound artificial.

## 🔴 HARD BAN

- leverage
- synergy
- ecosystem
- curated experience
- best-in-class
- value proposition
- innovative
- disruptive
- content strategy
- user engagement
- maximise engagement
- scalable
- stakeholders
- customer journey
- deliverables
- optimise
- activation
- brand experience
- premium offering

### Why

Nobody talks like this during a radio show.

It instantly destroys authenticity.

### Prefer

- show
- music
- requests
- community
- listeners
- countdown
- chart
- week

---

## 🟡 SOFT AVOID

- community-driven
- listener-powered

These are acceptable occasionally, but **"your requests"** usually sounds more natural.

---

# 2. Overstated Chart Claims

The Top 10 is important **within Muddy's**.

Do not pretend it represents the wider music industry.

---

## 🔴 HARD BAN

- the biggest chart in music
- the hottest chart
- the world's biggest countdown
- the biggest song in the world
- breaking records
- chart phenomenon
- global sensation
- viral smash
- internet-breaking
- everyone's talking about...
- taking the world by storm

---

## 🔴 HARD BAN

Comparisons to:

- Billboard
- Official Charts
- ARIA
- Spotify Charts
- Apple Music Charts

unless explicitly discussing them as factual context.

---

## 🟡 SOFT AVOID

- massive hit
- huge success
- unstoppable
- dominates the music world

Only acceptable if clearly referring **within the Muddy's chart**, not globally.

---

## Prefer

- another strong week
- continues to climb
- building momentum
- proving popular around Muddy's
- earning more requests
- one of this week's stories

---

# 3. Fabricated Facts

If the data doesn't prove it...

Don't write it.

---

## 🔴 HARD BAN

Inventing:

- artist history
- chart history
- previous releases
- Grammy wins
- ARIA wins
- BRIT Awards
- interviews
- fan reactions
- streaming numbers
- radio play
- career milestones
- release dates
- producer information
- record labels

unless supplied.

---

## 🔴 HARD BAN

Statements like:

> Their biggest hit since...

> Their highest charting single...

> Their fans are loving...

> Following their Grammy win...

> Critics are calling...

> Destined for number one...

> Surely next week's winner...

---

## 🔴 HARD BAN

Historical comparisons unless calculated.

Examples:

- longest run
- biggest climber ever
- highest debut
- first artist since...

---

## Prefer

Describe only what is known:

- current chart movement
- current position
- supplied statistics
- observed movement

---

---

## 🔴 HARD BAN

Talking down to listeners

Examples

- Obviously...

- Believe it or not...

---

## 🔴 HARD BAN

Trying to sound Gen Z

Examples

- slaps
- no cap
- bussin
- fire
- GOATed
- low key
- high key
- cooked
- cooked hard
- sigma
- aura

---

## 🟡 SOFT AVOID

Generic commercial radio

Examples

- after the break...
- stay tuned...

---

## 🟡 SOFT AVOID

Newsreader style

"This week's chart sees the continued dominance of..."

# 5. Second Life Mistakes

These are particularly important.

Mistakes here damage credibility immediately.

---

## 🔴 HARD BAN

Calling Second Life:

- a game
- an MMO
- a virtual game
- online game

Correct:

Virtual world.

---

## 🔴 HARD BAN

Calling residents:

- players
- gamers
- users

Prefer

- residents
- listeners
- everyone inworld

---

## 🔴 HARD BAN

Talking about:

- levelling up
- quests
- NPCs
- servers
- game maps

---

## 🔴 HARD BAN

Assuming avatars are fictional.

Treat avatars as people.

---

## 🟡 SOFT AVOID

Repeatedly explaining SL terms.

Regular listeners already know:

- SLT
- TP
- inworld

Don't explain every week.

# 6. Content Boundaries

## 🔴 HARD BAN

Politics

Religion

Culture-war topics

Real-world tragedies

Conspiracy theories

Medical advice

Financial advice

Legal advice

Current affairs unrelated to music

---

## 🔴 HARD BAN

Adult content

Explicit sexual language

Strong profanity

Drug promotion

Gambling promotion

Violence

---

## 🟡 SOFT AVOID

Alcohol references

Only acceptable if:

- part of song title
- obvious joke
- venue atmosphere

Never encourage drinking.

---

## 🟡 SOFT AVOID

Religious references

Only where directly relevant to music.

---

# 7. Structural Mistakes

These are editorial errors rather than writing style.

---

## 🔴 HARD BAN

Revealing Number One

before the intended reveal.

---

## 🔴 HARD BAN

Listing all ten songs

inside a Facebook or Discord post.

The graphic already does this.

---

## 🔴 HARD BAN

Mixing weeks.

Never confuse:

- last week's chart
- this week's chart

---

## 🔴 HARD BAN

Inventing listener requests.

Never write:

"Thanks to Sarah's request..."

unless explicitly supplied.

---

## 🔴 HARD BAN

Inventing genres.

Avoid:

- pop anthem
- indie classic
- country smash

unless supplied.

---

## 🔴 HARD BAN

Inventing chart statistics.

Never estimate.

# 9. Repetition Rules

The AI has a habit of repeating favourite phrases.

Avoid using within the same document:

- building momentum
- another strong week
- your requests
- holds firm
- quietly climbing

more than once or twice.

Vary naturally.

---

# 10. Punctuation & Formatting

## 🔴 HARD BAN

Multiple punctuation

!!!!

????

...

used excessively

---

## 🔴 HARD BAN

ALL CAPS

except:

NEW

RETURNS

HOLDS

UP

DOWN

---

## 🟡 SOFT AVOID

Emoji overload.

One or two is enough.

---

# 11. False Drama

The chart should never feel more dramatic than it really is.

Avoid writing:

- the most dramatic chart ever
- history has been made
- unbelievable scenes
- nobody saw this coming
- earth-shattering
- game-changing
- shocking result

Unless the supplied data genuinely supports it.

---

# 12. Hallucination Prevention

When information is missing:

**Never guess.**

Never infer:

- release dates
- artist history
- genre
- chart history
- previous winners
- future outcomes
- listener motivations

Instead:

Write about what **is** known.

---

# 13. Final Editorial Test

Before publishing, every piece of AI-generated copy should pass these questions.

## Authenticity

Could DJ Toohey comfortably read this aloud?

If not...

Rewrite it.

---

## Community

Does this sound like it belongs to Muddy's Music Cafe?

Or could it have come from any radio station?

---

## Credibility

Is every factual statement supported by supplied data?

---

## Humility

Does the copy celebrate the community...

without pretending to be the Official Charts?

---

## Story

Does the writing explain **why this week's chart matters**...

rather than simply trying to manufacture excitement?

---

# Golden Rule

> **Never write something because it sounds exciting.**
>
> **Write it because it's true.**
>
> The excitement comes from the chart itself, the music, and the community—not from exaggerated language.
