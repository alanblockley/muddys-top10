# Muddy's Music Cafe & Second Life Reference Guide

> **Purpose**
>
> This document provides stable background context for AI systems generating content for the **Muddy's Music Cafe Top 10** and other Muddy's communications.
>
> It explains the venue, community, Second Life terminology and culture, and how the weekly chart fits into listener behaviour. This document intentionally excludes weekly chart data, presenters' personalities, and feature-specific details so it remains stable over time.

---

# Venue Profile

## What is Muddy's Music Cafe?

Muddy's Music Cafe is a long-established live music venue in **Second Life** that blends the feel of a relaxed country pub, music café and community social club.

Although "Cafe" appears in the name, Muddy's is best thought of as a welcoming live music venue where people gather to enjoy DJs, discover music, spend time with friends and become part of an active community.

The venue is not built around roleplay, nightclub culture or contests.

Instead, it is built around:

- Music
- Community
- Conversation
- Entertainment
- Regular weekly programming

The emphasis is on creating an environment where people stay for hours rather than simply arriving for a few songs before moving elsewhere.

---

## Atmosphere

The atmosphere is warm, friendly and inclusive.

People come to Muddy's to:

- enjoy music
- socialise
- request songs
- participate in games and features
- spend time with friends
- discover new music

Humour, conversation and audience interaction are just as important as the playlist.

The culture is:

- relaxed rather than high-energy
- welcoming rather than exclusive
- community-driven rather than anonymous
- social rather than performance-focused

Regular visitors know one another by avatar name, while newcomers are welcomed naturally into the community.

---

## Physical Style

Muddy's has the appearance of a rustic country music venue rather than a modern nightclub.

Typical characteristics include:

- timber construction
- warm lighting
- comfortable seating
- open gathering spaces
- dance floor
- DJ booth/performance area
- natural outdoor surroundings

The venue feels comfortable and lived-in rather than flashy or futuristic.

When describing Muddy's, think:

> **Friendly local music venue**

not

> **Virtual nightclub.**

---

## What Makes Muddy's Different?

The strongest differentiator is its community.

Many Second Life venues focus primarily on dancing, contests, tipping or rapid DJ changes.

Muddy's focuses on:

- long-running relationships between DJs and listeners
- recurring weekly programmes
- audience participation
- music discovery
- radio-style presentation
- listener involvement through requests

Listeners become part of the entertainment through conversation, games, requests and recurring community features.

The venue has developed its own traditions and identity that regular patrons recognise from week to week.

# Second Life Context Rules

## What is Second Life?

Second Life is a **virtual world**.

Within the community it is often simply referred to as:

- the grid
- the Grid

depending on context.

Never describe Second Life as a game.

---

## Inworld

"Inworld" means being logged into and present within Second Life.

Examples:

- Join us inworld.
- See you inworld.
- Live inworld from Muddy's Music Cafe.

Do not write:

- inside the game
- while playing Second Life
- in the game world

---

## Logging In

Residents:

- log in to Second Life

After logging in they:

- visit Muddy's
- attend events
- go to Muddy's
- spend time with friends
- enjoy live music

Treat Muddy's exactly as you would a real-world venue.

---

## Teleporting

Travel is referred to as **teleporting** or **TPing**.

Residents generally TP by following a **SLURL (Second Life URL)**.

Natural language:

- TP over to Muddy's.
- Grab the SLURL.
- Follow the SLURL.

Avoid gaming terminology such as:

- spawn
- fast travel
- level
- map loading

---

## SLT

SLT means **Second Life Time**.

It generally aligns with the Pacific Time zone used by Linden Lab.

Within the community simply writing:

> Saturday 3am SLT

is sufficient.

Do not repeatedly explain what SLT means unless writing specifically for newcomers.

---

## Residents and Avatars

Avatars represent real people.

Treat residents as people, not fictional characters.

Natural language:

- listeners
- residents
- community members
- people

Avoid language that separates the avatar from the person unnecessarily.

# Audience Profile

## Who the Chart Is For

The Muddy's Music Cafe Top 10 is created specifically for the Muddy's community.

It reflects the music played at Muddy's throughout the week and the songs requested by its listeners.

The audience should be thought of as a community of regular patrons rather than a general internet audience.

---

## Audience Size

Typical live attendance:

- 30–100 residents inworld at Muddy's

The wider Second Life grid typically has:

- approximately 10,000–65,000 residents online

The internet radio stream usually attracts significantly more listeners than are physically present at the venue, commonly around **66% more** than the inworld audience.

Many listeners enjoy the show remotely without being present at Muddy's itself.

---

## Community Culture

The audience feels much more like:

- a local pub crowd
- a group of friends

than

- a mainstream radio audience

Many listeners know one another by avatar name.

Music brings people together, but conversation and entertainment keep them returning.

---

## Why People Attend

People attend Muddy's to:

- enjoy live music
- socialise
- discover songs
- request favourites
- participate in community activities
- spend time with friends

The music and the community are equally important.

---

## Geographic Reach

The community is international.

The live show is primarily presented to audiences across:

- United States
- United Kingdom
- Australia

However, recap content and social media reach a global audience.

Never assume a particular country or time zone.

---

## Music Policy

Music played at Muddy's generally follows these guidelines:

- 1970s to present
- Pop
- Soft Rock
- PG radio-friendly

Tracks should have charted in either:

- US Billboard Hot 100
- UK Top 40

This is an editorial policy rather than simply a playlist preference.

# How the Chart Connects to Listener Behaviour

## How the Chart Is Compiled

The preferred explanation is:

> **The Muddy's Music Cafe Top 10 is compiled from songs played by our DJs throughout the week and your requests.**

This wording should be used consistently.

---

## Listener Influence

Listeners do not vote.

Instead, they influence the chart by requesting songs during DJ sets.

When a requested song is played, that play contributes to the weekly chart.

Useful wording:

- Your requests help shape next week's chart.
- Every request could help move a song up the countdown.
- Hear something you love? Request it during the week.

Never describe the chart as being based on voting or polling.

---

## Counting Plays

Every qualifying DJ play carries equal weight.

A song may appear multiple times during the week provided the plays are not consecutive.

A back-to-back repeat counts as a repeat and is not counted separately.

This technical rule generally does not need explaining in public-facing content.

---

## Scope of the Chart

The chart measures only music played at Muddy's Music Cafe.

It is not:

- a Second Life-wide chart
- a streaming chart
- a voting chart
- a popularity poll

It is specifically the weekly chart of music played at Muddy's Music Cafe.

# Show Format Summary

## Overview

The **Muddy's Music Cafe Top 10** is the headline feature within a three-hour live radio-style DJ programme.

Rather than beginning immediately with the countdown, the show spends its opening hours building anticipation through music, conversation and entertainment before the chart begins.

---

## Schedule

**Every Saturday**

- Live show: **1:00am–4:00am SLT**
- Top 10 countdown: **approximately 3:00am SLT**
- Countdown duration: **30–40 minutes**

Promotion should encourage listeners to join from the beginning of the show rather than only for the countdown.

---

## Countdown Structure

Normally:

- Countdown runs from #10 to #1.
- All ten chart tracks are played in full.
- Every track receives presenter commentary.
- Songs are generally played in pairs, with commentary before the first and after the second.
- A brief recap may occur around Number 5.

Shortened countdowns are rare exceptions.

---

## Other Programme Features

The wider programme may also include recurring features such as:

- Alphabet Soup
- Track of the Week

These are independent programme features and should never be described as part of the Top 10 itself.

---

## Presentation

The live show is presented by:

- DJ Toohey
- JP (host)

Together they are known as **The Australia Dynamic Duo**.

JP should normally be referenced when promoting the live show but does not need to appear in every chart-related social media post.

# Cross-Platform Considerations

## Inworld

Assume the audience:

- already knows Muddy's
- understands Second Life terminology
- understands SLT
- recognises community members
- expects conversational language

Use natural community terminology such as:

- inworld
- TP
- SLURL
- SLT

without unnecessary explanation.

---

## Social Media

The audience is broader.

Some readers may never have visited Muddy's or even Second Life.

Provide enough context to make the content understandable without over-explaining.

Prioritise:

- the music
- the community
- the story behind the chart

rather than Second Life terminology.

Use Second Life-specific terms only where they add value or are essential.

---

# Hard Rules

## Never Assume

- Second Life is a game.
- Residents are roleplaying unless explicitly stated.
- Avatars are fictional characters rather than real people.
- Everyone listening is physically present at Muddy's.
- Everyone is in the same country or time zone.
- The chart is based on voting.
- The chart represents all of Second Life.
- Listener requests are weighted differently from DJ plays.
- The Top 10 is the entire three-hour show.
- Alphabet Soup or Track of the Week are part of the Top 10 countdown.

---

## Always Assume

- Muddy's is a genuine community.
- Listeners influence the chart through requests.
- Music and community are equally important.
- The audience values friendly conversation as much as music.
- The venue should be described like a real-world music venue.
- Second Life terminology should sound natural to residents.
- Promotional content should encourage listeners to join the show early and build anticipation for the countdown.
