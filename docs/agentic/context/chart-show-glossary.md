# AI Reference: Radio Chart Show Glossary

> **Purpose**
>
> This glossary defines common terminology used in radio chart countdowns, music programming and broadcast production. Definitions are intentionally concise and written for use by AI systems generating natural-sounding chart show content.

---

# Chart Terminology

## Chart Position
**Definition:** A song's numerical ranking within the chart.

**Example:**
> "This week, Taylor Swift climbs to chart position number two."

---

## Chart Placing
**Definition:** Another term for a song's ranking or position within the chart.

**Example:**
> "Its highest chart placing so far is number four."

---

## New Entry
**Definition:** A song appearing in the chart for the first time.

**Example:**
> "A brand new entry at number eight."

---

## Debut
**Definition:** The first chart appearance of a song or artist.

**Example:**
> "The single makes its chart debut this week."

---

## Climber
**Definition:** A song that has moved higher compared to the previous chart.

**Example:**
> "One of this week's biggest climbers is Bruno Mars."

---

## Riser
**Definition:** Another term for a climbing song.

**Example:**
> "A steady riser over the past month."

---

## Faller
**Definition:** A song moving down the chart.

**Example:**
> "Last week's runner-up becomes this week's biggest faller."

---

## Slider
**Definition:** Informal term describing a song that has dropped positions.

**Example:**
> "One of the week's notable sliders."

---

## Non-Mover
**Definition:** A song occupying exactly the same chart position as the previous week.

**Example:**
> "A non-mover at number five."

---

## Holds
**Definition:** Broadcast shorthand indicating a song remains in the same position.

**Example:**
> "It holds at number three for another week."

---

## Re-entry
**Definition:** A song returning to the chart after previously leaving.

**Example:**
> "A surprise re-entry at number nine."

---

## Return
**Definition:** Another way of describing a re-entry.

**Example:**
> "The song returns to the Top 10 after six weeks away."

---

## Peak Position
**Definition:** The highest position a song has reached.

**Example:**
> "Its peak position remains number two."

---

## New Peak
**Definition:** The highest chart position a song has achieved to date.

**Example:**
> "A new peak this week as it climbs to number four."

---

## Weeks on Chart
**Definition:** The total number of weeks a song has spent in the chart.

**Example:**
> "Now spending its 18th week on the chart."

---

## Chart Run
**Definition:** The complete sequence of weeks a song spends in the chart.

**Example:**
> "It's enjoying an impressive chart run."

---

## Consecutive Weeks at #1
**Definition:** The number of uninterrupted weeks a song has remained at number one.

**Example:**
> "That's four consecutive weeks at number one."

---

## Knocked Off
**Definition:** Describes a song losing the number one position.

**Example:**
> "It was finally knocked off the top spot."

---

## Dethroned
**Definition:** A dramatic way of saying a chart leader has been replaced.

**Example:**
> "After six weeks, it's been dethroned."

---

## Bubble
**Definition:** Songs sitting just outside the published chart.

**Example:**
> "Watch out next week—it was bubbling just outside the Top 10."

---

## Straight In
**Definition:** Entering the chart at a high position without previous appearances.

**Example:**
> "Straight in at number three."

---

## Hot Shot
**Definition:** The week's fastest-rising song.

**Example:**
> "This week's Hot Shot jumps 15 places."

---

## Movement Delta
**Definition:** The numerical change in chart position compared to the previous week.

**Example:**
> "The movement delta is +8."

---

# Radio Broadcast Terminology

## Cue
**Definition:** The exact point where audio should begin.

**Example:**
> "Cue the next track on my count."

---

## Out-Cue
**Definition:** The point where speech or audio should end.

**Example:**
> "Finish your link before the vocal out-cue."

---

## Back-Announce (Back-Anno)
**Definition:** Identifying a song after it has finished playing.

**Example:**
> "That was Alex Warren with *FEVER DREAM*, climbing to number four."

---

## Segue
**Definition:** A smooth transition between songs or speech.

**Example:**
> "Let's segue from that into this week's biggest climber."

---

## Bed
**Definition:** Background music played underneath speech.

**Example:**
> "Keep the bed low while introducing the chart."

---

## Sting
**Definition:** A very short branded audio identifier.

**Example:**
> "Top 10 Countdown... coming up!"

---

## Jingle
**Definition:** A produced musical branding element.

**Example:**
> "Here's your Muddy's Music Cafe Top 10!"

---

## Talk-Up
**Definition:** Speaking over the instrumental introduction of a song before the vocals begin.

**Example:**
> "I'll talk this one up to the first lyric."

---

## Link
**Definition:** Spoken content between two pieces of audio.

**Example:**
> "Here's a quick link before number six."

---

## Sweep
**Definition:** Playing multiple songs consecutively with little or no presenter interruption.

**Example:**
> "We'll sweep through the next three songs."

---

## Format Clock
**Definition:** The planned timing structure for a programme.

**Example:**
> "The format clock has the ad break after number five."

---

## Drop
**Definition:** A very short audio clip, often featuring a voice or sound effect.

**Example:**
> "Play the countdown drop before the reveal."

---

## Liner
**Definition:** A short pre-recorded station or programme identifier.

**Example:**
> "'Your Requests. Your Music. Your Chart.' is the show's signature liner."

---

## Rotation
**Definition:** How frequently a station schedules a track.

**Example:**
> "It's currently in medium rotation."

---

## Power Play
**Definition:** A song receiving the highest frequency of airplay.

**Example:**
> "This week's Power Play is in heavy rotation."

---

## Heavy Rotation
**Definition:** Frequent scheduled airplay throughout the broadcast day.

**Example:**
> "The single enters heavy rotation this week."

---

## Breaker
**Definition:** A new song being tested with limited airplay before wider rotation.

**Example:**
> "It's currently a breaker on the playlist."

---

# Show Structure Terms

## Countdown
**Definition:** A programme revealing chart positions from lowest to highest.

**Example:**
> "Welcome to this week's Top 10 countdown."

---

## Rundown
**Definition:** A sequential listing of chart positions.

**Example:**
> "Let's run through today's rundown."

---

## Recap
**Definition:** A summary of positions already revealed.

**Example:**
> "Here's a quick recap of numbers ten through six."

---

## Tease
**Definition:** A brief preview of upcoming content.

**Example:**
> "Still to come—the biggest climber of the week."

---

## Tease Forward
**Definition:** Looking ahead to encourage listeners to stay tuned.

**Example:**
> "We'll reveal the new number one after this."

---

## Reveal
**Definition:** The announcement of a chart position.

**Example:**
> "Time to reveal this week's number three."

---

## Build-Up
**Definition:** Deliberately increasing anticipation before a reveal.

**Example:**
> "Only two songs remain..."

---

## Payoff
**Definition:** The satisfying conclusion following the build-up.

**Example:**
> "After all that anticipation, here's your number one."

---

## Cold Open
**Definition:** Beginning the programme immediately without a formal introduction.

**Example:**
> "Straight into number ten after the show opener."

---

## Outro
**Definition:** The closing remarks of a programme.

**Example:**
> "That's this week's countdown—see you next Saturday."

---

## Sign-Off
**Definition:** The presenter's final farewell.

**Example:**
> "I'm DJ Toohey. Thanks for listening."

---

# Social & Promotional Terms

## Chart Drop
**Definition:** The moment the latest chart is released publicly.

**Example:**
> "The chart drops every Saturday at 2am SLT."

---

## Spoiler
**Definition:** Information revealing chart results before the official countdown.

**Example:**
> "No spoilers—we'll reveal the winner live."

---

## Chart Graphic
**Definition:** A visual representation of the chart rankings.

**Example:**
> "Download this week's chart graphic."

---

## Chart Card
**Definition:** A social-media-friendly image summarising chart information.

**Example:**
> "Share this week's Top 10 chart card."

---

## Engagement Hook
**Definition:** A line designed to encourage comments, reactions or shares.

**Example:**
> "Which song should be number one next week?"

---

## CTA (Call to Action)
**Definition:** A direct instruction encouraging audience participation.

**Example:**
> "Drop your requests in the comments and tune in next Saturday!"

---

## Alt Text
**Definition:** A written description of an image used for accessibility.

**Example:**
> "Alt text: Purple-themed Top 10 chart graphic showing Ella Langley at number one with The Weeknd as the biggest climber."
