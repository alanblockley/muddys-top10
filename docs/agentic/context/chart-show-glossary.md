# AI Reference: Chart Show Glossary

> **Purpose**
>
> This glossary defines terminology used when writing about music charts — for radio read copy, social posts, and infographic editorial. Definitions are concise and written for use by AI systems generating natural-sounding chart content.

---

# Chart Terminology

## Chart Position
A song's numerical ranking within the chart.
> "This week, Taylor Swift climbs to chart position number two."

## New Entry
A song appearing in the chart for the first time.
> "A brand new entry at number eight."

## Debut
The first chart appearance of a song or artist.
> "The single makes its chart debut this week."

## Climber / Riser
A song that has moved higher compared to the previous chart.
> "One of this week's biggest climbers is Bruno Mars."

## Faller / Slider
A song moving down the chart.
> "Last week's runner-up becomes this week's biggest faller."

## Non-Mover
A song occupying exactly the same chart position as the previous week.
> "A non-mover at number five."

## Holds
Shorthand indicating a song remains in the same position.
> "It holds at number three for another week."

## Re-entry / Return
A song returning to the chart after previously leaving.
> "A surprise re-entry at number nine."

## Peak Position
The highest position a song has reached.
> "Its peak position remains number two."

## New Peak
The highest chart position a song has achieved to date.
> "A new peak this week as it climbs to number four."

## Weeks on Chart
The total number of weeks a song has spent in the chart.
> "Now spending its 18th week on the chart."

## Chart Run
The complete sequence of weeks a song spends in the chart.
> "It's enjoying an impressive chart run."

## Consecutive Weeks at #1
The number of uninterrupted weeks a song has remained at number one.
> "That's four consecutive weeks at number one."

## Knocked Off / Dethroned
A song losing the number one position.
> "After six weeks, it's been dethroned."

## Bubble
Songs sitting just outside the published chart.
> "Watch out next week — it was bubbling just outside the Top 10."

## Straight In
Entering the chart at a high position without previous appearances.
> "Straight in at number three."

## Hot Shot
The week's fastest-rising song.
> "This week's Hot Shot jumps 15 places."

## Movement Delta
The numerical change in chart position compared to the previous week.
> "The movement delta is +8."

# Social & Promotional Terms

## Chart Drop
The moment the latest chart is released publicly.
> "The chart drops every Saturday at 2am SLT."

## Spoiler
Information revealing chart results before the official countdown.
> "No spoilers — we'll reveal the winner live."

## Chart Graphic / Chart Card
A visual representation of the chart rankings for social media.
> "Share this week's Top 10 chart card."

## Engagement Hook
A line designed to encourage comments, reactions or shares.
> "Which song should be number one next week?"

## CTA (Call to Action)
A direct instruction encouraging audience participation.
> "Drop your requests in the comments and tune in next Saturday!"

## Alt Text
A written description of an image used for accessibility.
> "Alt text: Purple-themed Top 10 chart graphic showing Ella Langley at number one with The Weeknd as the biggest climber."
