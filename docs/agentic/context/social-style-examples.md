# Muddy's Music Cafe Top 10
# Social Media Editorial Style Guide

> **Purpose**
>
> This document defines the editorial style for promoting the **Muddy's Music Cafe Top 10** across social media. It covers the written copy that accompanies the weekly chart infographic and live show promotions. Visual design is documented separately.
>
> Every platform has its own audience and expectations, but every post should still sound recognisably like **DJ Toohey's Top 10**.

---

# Editorial Philosophy

The social post should never simply announce that a chart exists.

Instead, it should answer one question:

> **"Why should someone care about this week's chart?"**

The infographic contains the detail.

The post provides the story.

The objective is curiosity, not spoilers.

---

# Universal Principles

Every platform should feel:

- Community-first
- Broadcast-inspired
- Conversational
- Confident
- Friendly

Never:

- Clickbait
- Corporate
- Generic radio
- AI generated
- Overly dramatic

The chart belongs to the listeners.

Always reinforce:

- Your requests
- Our DJs
- Our community

---

# Facebook

## Audience

Facebook reaches both regular Muddy's listeners and people who may never have visited the venue.

Posts should therefore provide enough context without assuming prior knowledge.

---

## Typical Structure

Hook

↓

Chart Story

↓

Invitation / CTA

↓

Graphic

---

## Length

Ideal:

120–250 words

Usually:

3–6 short paragraphs.

White space matters.

Avoid walls of text.

---

## Relationship to the Graphic

The graphic contains:

- rankings
- movement
- statistics

The post should avoid repeating those.

Instead explain:

Why this week matters.

---

# Golden Example 1

Another Saturday means another chapter in the Muddy's Music Cafe Top 10.

This week's chart has been one of the closest we've seen in a while, with a new number one finally breaking through after weeks of building momentum. We've also welcomed a brand new entry and seen some familiar favourites refuse to give up their place.

Your requests and our DJs shape this chart every single week, and that's what makes every countdown different.

Check out this week's chart below, then join us live this Saturday from **2am SLT** as JP and I count it all down together.

**Your requests. Your music. Your chart.**

### Why it works

- Starts with the week's story.
- Doesn't list chart positions.
- Ends with a clear invitation.

---

# Golden Example 2

Sometimes the biggest stories aren't the biggest jumps.

This week is proof of that.

One track finally reaches the summit after weeks of knocking on the door, while another proves that consistency can be just as powerful as momentum.

The full chart is below.

We'll tell the whole story live this Saturday at Muddy's Music Cafe.

### Why it works

Creates curiosity.

Doesn't spoil everything.

---

# Golden Example 3

The latest Muddy's Music Cafe Top 10 has arrived!

Another fantastic week of requests, another great mix of familiar favourites and fresh challengers, and another reminder that this really is *your* chart.

Take a look at this week's movers below, then come and hear the full countdown live this Saturday from 2am SLT.

### Why it works

Friendly.

Community focused.

Natural CTA.

```

```

---

## Engagement

Encourage conversation naturally.

Good prompts:

- Which track surprised you most?
- Which song do you think deserved to climb higher?
- What are you hoping to hear next week?

Avoid:

"COMMENT BELOW!!"

or

"SMASH THAT LIKE BUTTON"

---

## Hashtags

Very light.

Maximum:

2–5.

Examples:

#MuddysMusicCafe

#Top10

#SecondLife

#InternetRadio

Avoid large blocks of hashtags.

Be shorter.

↓

↓

## Discord Formatting

Use:

- bold headings
- occasional emoji
- channel formatting

Avoid:

Long essays.

---

## Mentions

Only mention:

@Top10

or

@Events

when genuinely appropriate.

Avoid unnecessary pings.

Community-first.

Assume people understand:

- Muddy's

More community focused.

# Pre-show Teasers

## Purpose

Build anticipation.

Not reveal results.

---

## Timing

Primary teaser:

12–24 hours before.

Reminder:

1–3 hours before.

---

## Golden Example 1

Only a few hours to go...

This week's chart has been one of the closest in recent memory.

One artist has finally broken through, we've got a brand new entry, and the race for number one came right down to the wire.

Join us from **2am SLT** to hear how it all unfolded.

---

## Golden Example 2

Coming up tonight...

The latest Muddy's Music Cafe Top 10.

Ten songs.

One number one.

And plenty of stories in between.

---

## Spoiler Rule

Reveal:

- there is a story

Never reveal:

- who won

---

# Post-show / Chart Drop

## Purpose

Celebrate.

Encourage sharing.

Drive discussion.

---

## Golden Example 1

This week's chart is officially here!

Congratulations to everyone whose favourites made the countdown, and thanks to everyone whose requests helped shape another fantastic week of music.

Missed the live show?

The full chart is below.

---

## Golden Example 2

Another countdown complete.

Another number one crowned.

Take a look at this week's chart and let us know what surprised you most.

---

## Number One Policy

The graphic naturally reveals the winner.

The text should not spend its opening sentence announcing it.

Instead:

Encourage people to view the chart.

---

# Cross Promotion

Cross-promotion is welcome but should feel organic.

Examples:

- "Join the discussion on Discord after the show."
- "Catch the chart on Facebook if you missed it."
- "See the latest infographic on Primfeed."

Avoid turning every post into a list of links.

One platform should have one primary call to action.

---

# Weeks Without Big Stories

Not every chart has dramatic movement.

That's fine.

Focus instead on:

- consistency
- close battles
- familiar favourites
- listener influence
- long-term momentum

Examples:

"Consistency wins again this week..."

"Another incredibly close finish..."

"The race at the top remains wide open..."

Never invent drama.

# Capitalisation

Headlines:

Sentence case or Title Case.

Never ALL CAPS.

Badges:

ALL CAPS.

Body:

Sentence case.

---

# Pairing Copy with the Graphic

The graphic answers:

"What happened?"

The post answers:

"Why does it matter?"

Avoid describing everything visible in the image.

Instead, encourage the reader to explore it.

The two should complement one another rather than duplicate the same information.

---

# Final Editorial Voice

Every social post should feel like it was written by someone who has just stepped away from the microphone after another Saturday morning at Muddy's Music Cafe.

Warm.

Community-driven.

Confident.

Never trying too hard.

The music is important.

The chart is important.

But the people behind both are what make the story worth telling.
