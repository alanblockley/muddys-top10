# Prompt: Social Media for Music Communities

**Target file:** `docs/agentic/context/social-media-music-communities.md`

---

## Paste this into ChatGPT:

```
I'm building an AI system that writes social media posts promoting a weekly music chart show. The show exists within a tight-knit online music community (not mainstream radio — more like a community radio/internet radio scene). The AI needs to understand what makes music community social posts engaging.

Please write a reference document covering:

## 1. What Drives Engagement in Music Communities
- Why chart reveals create natural anticipation and FOMO
- The "did you see?" effect — making people feel they'll miss something if they don't check
- Community pride when "their" artist or request makes the chart
- The debate/discussion hook ("do you agree with #1?", "what should be higher?")
- Nostalgia when returning tracks reappear
- The personal connection between listener requests and chart results

## 2. Post Structure That Works
- Hook line (first 1-2 sentences that appear in feed previews)
- The information payload (chart highlights, key movements)
- The engagement driver (question, call to action, invitation to respond)
- Appropriate hashtag usage (too many = spam, zero = invisible)
- Post length by platform (Facebook: 100-250 words optimal, Discord: shorter/punchier)
- When to use emoji (sparingly for emphasis, not decoration)

## 3. Platform-Specific Conventions

### Facebook
- Longer-form posts perform better in groups/communities
- Image + text together (infographic shared with chart narrative)
- Questions drive comments
- Tagging etiquette in community groups
- When to use line breaks for readability

### Discord
- Shorter, more immediate tone
- Embed-friendly links
- Spoiler tags for reveals if appropriate
- Reaction prompts ("react with 🎵 if you agree")
- Channel-appropriate tone (general vs music-specific)

### Short-Form / Teaser
- Pre-show teasers that build anticipation without spoiling
- "Coming this Saturday…" framing
- Cliffhanger style ("3 new entries shake things up…")

## 4. Music-Specific Social Conventions
- How to credit artists (name formatting, not @-tagging random accounts)
- Movement language that's visual in text ("🔥 straight in at #3!", "📈 climbs to a new peak!")
- Chart summaries that tell a story vs just listing positions
- The "chart card" post format (share infographic + caption with highlights)
- Spoiler etiquette (when is it ok to reveal #1 outside the show?)

## 5. Community Tone vs Brand Tone
- Why overly polished/corporate posts feel wrong in community spaces
- Conversational > professional (but still competent)
- Inside references that build belonging
- Celebrating the community ("YOUR requests made this happen")
- Inclusive language ("we", "our chart", "this week's community picks")

## 6. Calls to Action That Work
- Soft CTAs: "Catch the full countdown Saturday 2am"
- Engagement CTAs: "What's your pick for next week's #1?"
- Share CTAs: "Share if your request made the chart!"
- Platform-specific CTAs (Facebook comments vs Discord reactions)
- What NOT to do (desperate engagement bait, "like and subscribe" energy)

Write this as a reference document for an AI agent writing weekly social posts. Practical patterns and examples, not marketing theory. Markdown format.
```
