# Prompt: Infographic Style Examples

**Target file:** `docs/agentic/context/infographic-style-examples.md`

---

## Paste this into ChatGPT:

```
I need to document the editorial/copy style for my weekly chart infographic. This isn't about the visual design (that's handled elsewhere) — it's about the TEXT that goes on the infographic: headlines, chart story, movement badges, stats callouts, and footer copy.

The infographic is a 1280×720 PNG shared on Facebook, Discord, and Primfeed to promote the weekly Top 10 chart show at Muddy's Music Cafe.

Help me define the copy style. I'll provide examples or preferences, and you synthesise.

## What I need documented:

1. **Headline style**: The big text at the top. Is it the show name? The week's story? A hook?
   - Examples of headlines I've used or would use
   - Length constraint (how many characters fit visually)
   - Tone: factual ("Week of July 26") vs editorial ("New #1 shakes up the chart")

2. **Subhead/tagline**: Secondary text below the headline.
   - Fixed tagline ("Your requests. Your music. Your chart.") or variable?
   - Relationship to the headline

3. **Chart story** (1-2 sentence narrative callout):
   - How I summarise the week's story in a tight space
   - Examples: "After 3 weeks in the shadows, X rockets back to claim the top spot"
   - What makes a good chart story vs a bland summary

4. **Movement badge wording**: The short text on movement indicators
   - New entry: "NEW" / "NEW ENTRY" / "DEBUT" / "🔥 NEW"?
   - Climber: "UP 3" / "+3" / "⬆️ 3 PLACES"?
   - Faller: "DOWN 2" / "-2" / "⬇️"?
   - Non-mover: "HOLDS" / "=" / "NON-MOVER"?
   - Re-entry: "RETURNS" / "RE-ENTRY" / "BACK"?

5. **Statistics/callouts**: The supporting numbers section
   - Which stats to feature (total plays, new entries, biggest climber, longest run)
   - How they're labelled ("42 TRACKS PLAYED" vs "This Week: 42 plays")
   - Tone: data-forward or narrative-forward?

6. **Footer/promotion strip**: The bottom section driving action
   - Show time, venue, hosts
   - CTA ("Catch the countdown every Saturday")
   - What's fixed vs what varies

7. **What looks wrong**: Text that would look out of place on the graphic
   - Too wordy?
   - Too formal?
   - Wrong capitalisation style?

---

After I answer, synthesise into a markdown document with:
- Style rules per text element (headline, subhead, chart story, badges, stats, footer)
- Character/word limits for each
- 3-5 example headlines and chart stories I'd approve
- 3-5 examples I'd reject (with why)
- Capitalisation and punctuation rules
- Overall editorial tone for the graphic
```

---

## Notes

- If you have past infographics, describe the text on them or upload them to ChatGPT for it to extract patterns.
- The `samples/` directory in the project has reference infographic PNGs — you could screenshot the text and describe what you liked/didn't like.
- This bridges between the visual design principles (prompt 03) and the actual copy output.
