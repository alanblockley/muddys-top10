# Prompt: Never Say

**Target file:** `docs/agentic/context/never-say.md`

---

## Paste this into ChatGPT:

```
I need to build a "banned list" for an AI that writes content for my weekly Top 10 chart show at Muddy's Music Cafe. These are words, phrases, framing, claims, and tones that should NEVER appear in generated content.

This is the guardrail document. When the AI produces something and it "just sounds wrong" — this file explains why.

Help me build this by category. I'll explain what sounds wrong to me, you organise it.

## Categories:

### 1. Corporate/Marketing Speak
Words or phrases that sound like they came from a marketing department, not a DJ or community member:
- Examples I hate: (e.g., "leverage", "synergy", "curated experience", "content")
- What they should be replaced with
- Why they sound wrong in this context

### 2. Overstated Chart Claims
Things the AI shouldn't say because they overstate what a community radio Top 10 actually is:
- "The hottest chart in [anything]"
- "Breaking records"
- "The biggest track in the world right now"
- Comparisons to mainstream charts
- Claiming "viral" status
- What's the right level of enthusiasm? (Genuine community pride, not Billboard-level hype)

### 3. Fabricated Facts
Types of claims the AI should never make unless explicitly provided in the data:
- Artist career history ("their biggest hit since…")
- Industry context ("following their Grammy win…")
- Listener demographics ("fans everywhere are…")
- Chart predictions ("destined for #1 next week")
- Historical comparisons ("the longest run since…" — unless data proves it)

### 4. Wrong Tone
Tones/registers that don't fit:
- Too formal/newsreader ("This week's chart sees the continued dominance of…")
- Too edgy/shock-jock
- Too generic radio ("coming up after the break…" when there is no break)
- Condescending to the audience
- Over-explaining obvious things
- Trying too hard to be hip/young

### 5. Second Life Mistakes
Things that would embarrass us in the SL community:
- Calling it a "game"
- Assuming real-world geography applies
- Wrong terminology (what do outsiders get wrong?)
- Assumptions about avatars/real identities
- Referencing "logging in" instead of appropriate SL language

### 6. Content Boundaries
- Not PG-appropriate (what's the actual line?)
- Drug/alcohol references beyond what's appropriate
- Real-world politics
- Religious references
- Anything that could be mistaken for a real news claim

### 7. Structural Mistakes
Things that are technically content errors:
- Revealing #1 too early in a social post (spoiler)
- Listing all 10 tracks in a social post (defeats the purpose of the show)
- Using "last week" data as "this week" (temporal confusion)
- Attributing a request to a specific listener without data
- Assuming a track's genre or categorising music

### 8. Phrases That Sound "AI-Generated"
Specific phrases that are dead giveaways of AI writing:
- "Dive into"
- "Let's unpack"
- "It's not just [X], it's [Y]"
- "In the ever-evolving landscape of…"
- "Buckle up"
- Others you've noticed and hate?

---

After I answer, format as a strict reference document with:
- Category headers
- Specific banned phrases/words (in a list)
- Why each is banned (brief note)
- What to use instead (where applicable)
- Severity levels: HARD BAN (never ever) vs SOFT AVOID (use sparingly if at all)
```

---

## Notes

- Interactive — needs your specific pet peeves and boundaries.
- Start with the obvious ones, then add over time as you review AI output and find things you hate.
- This is the most iterative file — it'll grow every time you review a campaign draft and think "ugh, not that."
- Cross-reference with `words-and-phrases.md` — things banned here shouldn't appear in the preferred vocabulary.
