# Prompt: Personal Voice

**Target file:** `docs/agentic/context/personal-voice.md`

---

## Paste this into ChatGPT:

```
I need you to help me document my writing/communication voice so an AI can replicate it. I'll share some context, and I'd like you to ask me targeted questions and then synthesise the answers into a structured voice profile.

Context: I (Alan) run a weekly Top 10 chart show at Muddy's Music Cafe in Second Life. I write social media posts promoting the chart, review AI-generated content, and occasionally write radio read scripts. I need an AI to write in my voice for these outputs.

Please interview me with the following questions (I'll answer, then you synthesise):

1. **Tone spectrum**: On a scale from "dry/deadpan" to "enthusiastic/excitable", where do you sit when writing about the chart?

2. **Humour**: Do you use humour? What kind? (self-deprecating, observational, dad jokes, sarcasm, wordplay?) Give an example if you can.

3. **Formality**: Are you more "hey everyone, big week!" or "This week's chart sees significant movement"? Or somewhere else?

4. **Sentence style**: Short and punchy? Long and flowing? Mixed? Do you use fragments?

5. **Directness**: Do you state things plainly ("Number one is X") or build up to them ("and the track that's been climbing all month finally takes the top spot…")?

6. **Personality markers**: What makes YOUR writing recognisably yours? Any verbal tics, recurring phrases, signature openers/closers?

7. **Emoji/formatting**: Do you use emoji in posts? How much? Do you use bold, caps, line breaks for emphasis?

8. **Enthusiasm level**: Are you the type to write "HUGE week!" or "solid chart this week"? How do you calibrate excitement?

9. **Community reference**: How much do you reference the community, listeners, requests? Is it "I" focused or "we" focused?

10. **What sounds wrong**: If an AI wrote something that was technically correct but didn't sound like you, what would tip you off? What would make you cringe?

---

After I answer these, please synthesise into a structured markdown document with:
- A 2-3 sentence "voice summary" at the top
- Specific do/don't rules
- Example phrases that sound like me
- Example phrases that DON'T sound like me
- Notes on how my voice shifts by context (social post vs radio script vs casual)
```

---

## Notes

- This is interactive — you need to answer ChatGPT's questions from your own perspective.
- If you have past social posts or chart announcements you've written manually, paste a few in as reference material. ChatGPT can extract voice patterns from examples.
- The output should capture YOUR specific voice, not a generic "friendly DJ" voice.
