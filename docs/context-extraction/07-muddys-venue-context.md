# Prompt: Muddy's Venue Context

**Target file:** `docs/agentic/context/muddys-venue-context.md`

---

## Paste this into ChatGPT:

```
I need to document the context around Muddy's Music Cafe for use by an AI that generates weekly chart show content. Muddy's is a venue in Second Life where the Top 10 chart show happens. The AI needs stable background context so it doesn't make incorrect assumptions.

Help me capture this. I'll answer your questions, then you synthesise.

1. **What is Muddy's Music Cafe?** Describe it — is it a bar? Club? Lounge? What's the vibe? What does it look like?

2. **Second Life context**: What does the AI need to know about Second Life to write appropriately? What's an "inworld" event? What's SLT (Second Life Time)? What would sound ignorant if the AI got it wrong?

3. **The audience**: Who listens? How many typically? Are they "regulars" with names you'd recognise? Is it a tight community or casual drop-ins? What's the demographic/interest profile?

4. **How the chart works (listener-facing)**: How do listener requests become chart entries? Is it purely DJ playout? Requests weighted? How does the audience understand their influence on the chart?

5. **The show format**: When does it air? How long? Is it a dedicated chart show or part of a longer set? Do tracks get played during the countdown or just announced?

6. **Recurring events/rituals**: Are there regular events beyond the chart show? Weekly DJ nights? Special occasions? Things the AI should be aware of but not confuse with the chart?

7. **Community language**: What terms/phrases does the community use that an outsider wouldn't know? "TP" (teleport)? "Inworld"? Stream/sim/parcel? Muddy's-specific terms?

8. **Relationship between online and "inworld"**: The chart is announced on social media AND inworld. How do these two audiences differ? Does the same content work for both?

9. **What the AI should NEVER assume**: Things about Second Life, Muddy's, or the community that would be embarrassing to get wrong. (e.g., don't assume it's a "game", don't assume real names, don't assume everyone is in the same timezone)

10. **JP's role**: Who is JP? Co-host? Producer? What's their relationship to the show and community?

---

After I answer, synthesise into a markdown document with:
- A "venue profile" section (what Muddy's is, physically and culturally)
- Second Life context rules (terminology, assumptions to avoid)
- Audience profile
- How the chart connects to listener behaviour
- Show format summary
- Community language glossary
- Cross-platform considerations (inworld vs social media)
- Hard rules (things never to assume or state)
```

---

## Notes

- Interactive — requires your knowledge of the venue and community.
- This is the "world-building" context that prevents the AI from writing something that sounds like it doesn't know the community exists.
- Keep it factual and stable — this shouldn't change week-to-week.
