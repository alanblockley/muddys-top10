# Prompt: Words and Phrases

**Target file:** `docs/agentic/context/words-and-phrases.md`

---

## Paste this into ChatGPT:

```
I need to build a vocabulary reference for an AI that writes content for my weekly Top 10 chart show at Muddy's Music Cafe. This is the "preferred language" guide — words and phrases that should appear naturally in generated content.

This isn't about rigid templates. It's about giving the AI a vocabulary that sounds like it belongs to this show.

Help me build this by category. For each category, I'll provide examples or preferences, and you organise them.

## Categories:

### 1. Chart Movement Language (how we talk about positions)
- New entries: what phrases do I use? ("brand new at number 5", "makes its debut", "straight in")
- Climbers: ("up 3 spots", "climbing the chart", "on the rise")
- Fallers: ("drops back", "slips a few places", "loses ground")
- Non-movers: ("holding steady", "stays put", "a non-mover")
- Re-entries: ("returns to the chart", "back after a break")
- #1 specific: ("takes the top spot", "claims number one", "the track everyone's talking about")

### 2. Show/Event Language (how we talk about the show itself)
- How do I refer to the show? ("the countdown", "this week's Top 10", "chart night")
- How do I refer to the chart? ("the chart", "this week's chart", "the Top 10")
- Time references: ("this Saturday", "every week", "join us at 2am SLT")
- Energy words I naturally use: ("big week", "massive", "shake-up", "solid", "quiet week")

### 3. Community Language (how we talk about listeners)
- How I address the audience: ("you", "everyone", "the community", "our listeners")
- Request acknowledgment: ("your requests", "community-powered", "listener-driven")
- Belonging language: ("our chart", "the Muddy's family", "your music")

### 4. Calls to Action (how we invite engagement)
- Tuning in: ("catch the countdown", "join us Saturday", "don't miss it")
- Engagement: ("what do you think?", "agree with #1?", "let us know")
- Sharing: ("share with a friend", "spread the word")

### 5. Connectors and Transitions (glue phrases)
- Between positions: ("moving on", "next up", "and at number…")
- Building tension: ("and now…", "the moment you've been waiting for…")
- Recaps: ("so far we've seen…", "that rounds out our…")

### 6. Descriptors I Actually Use
- For tracks: ("banger", "tune", "track", "anthem"? What feels right?)
- For chart performance: ("dominates", "holds on", "sneaks in", "storms the chart"?)
- For the show mood: ("wild week", "steady chart", "big movements"?)

---

For each category:
- List 5-10 preferred phrases I naturally use or would approve
- Star (⭐) the ones that are most "me"
- Note any that should only be used sparingly (reserved for big moments)

Format as a markdown vocabulary reference grouped by category, with usage notes.
```
