# Prompt: DJ Toohey Style

**Target file:** `docs/agentic/context/dj-toohey-style.md`

---

## Paste this into ChatGPT:

```
I need to document the on-air presenting style of DJ Toohey for use by an AI that writes radio chart show scripts. DJ Toohey hosts the weekly Top 10 countdown at Muddy's Music Cafe (a Second Life venue), co-hosting with JP.

Help me capture the presenting style. I'll answer your questions, then you synthesise.

1. **Opening style**: How does DJ Toohey typically open the show? Cold open into the chart? Chat first? Set the scene? Give an example of a natural-sounding show opener.

2. **Position introductions**: How does he introduce each chart position? Formal ("at number 7 this week…") or casual ("coming in at 7…")? Does the style change as you get higher up the chart?

3. **Movement commentary**: When a track climbs/falls/enters, how much does he say about it? Quick acknowledgment or extended commentary? Examples?

4. **Interaction with JP**: What role does JP play? Reactions? Commentary? Banter? How is JP referenced in the script — do they get speaking lines or is it more "JP agrees" / "JP's nodding"?

5. **Community references**: Does Toohey reference listeners by name? Mention who requested what? How much "shout out" culture is there?

6. **Energy arc**: How does the energy change from position 10 to position 1? Does Toohey get noticeably more excited, or is it a consistent vibe?

7. **Reveal style for #1**: How is the number one announced? Big build-up? Dramatic pause? Casual? "And you already know…" style?

8. **Catchphrases or recurring lines**: Any signature phrases? Opening lines? Closers? Transitions between positions?

9. **Pacing**: Does Toohey talk fast or take his time? Are reads long or punchy? How much silence/pause is used?

10. **What Toohey would NEVER say**: Things that would sound wrong coming from this presenter. Too formal? Too "radio announcer"? Anything specific?

---

After I answer, synthesise into a markdown document with:
- A "presenter profile" paragraph at the top
- Script conventions (how positions are introduced, transitions between positions)
- Relationship with co-host JP
- Community engagement patterns
- Energy mapping (positions 10→1)
- Example lines that sound like Toohey
- Example lines that DON'T sound like Toohey
- The #1 reveal pattern
```

---
