# Prompt: Social Style Examples

**Target file:** `docs/agentic/context/social-style-examples.md`

---

## Paste this into ChatGPT:

```
I need to document the social media posting style for my weekly Top 10 chart show at Muddy's Music Cafe (Second Life internet radio). The AI needs examples of posts that sound right for each platform.

I post to: Facebook (group/page), Discord (community server), and Primfeed (Second Life social network). I'll provide examples or describe what works, and you synthesise.

## For each platform, I need:

### Facebook
- 2-3 examples of chart announcement posts I've written (or would write)
- The typical structure (hook → content → CTA)
- Length that works (how many lines/paragraphs)
- How I use the infographic image alongside the text
- Hashtag approach (if any)
- Engagement patterns (do I ask questions? prompt comments?)

### Discord
- 2-3 examples of chart posts for the server
- How Discord tone differs from Facebook (shorter? more casual? more reaction-driven?)
- Do I use embeds, mentions, channel formatting?
- How much chart data vs teaser

### Primfeed (or Second Life social)
- How this differs from Facebook (more community-internal?)
- SL-specific language I'd use here that I wouldn't on Facebook
- Typical post style

### Pre-show Teasers
- Examples of posts BEFORE the show airs (building anticipation)
- How much I spoil vs tease
- Timing (how far in advance?)

### Post-show / Chart Drop
- Examples of the "chart is out!" announcement
- Do I reveal #1 immediately or drive people to the graphic?
- How I handle engagement after posting

---

Questions:

1. Show me 3-5 social posts you've written about the chart that you were happy with. (If you don't have them, describe what you'd write for a hypothetical chart week.)

2. Show me a post you'd reject — something an AI wrote that didn't sound right.

3. How do you handle weeks where the chart is "boring" (no big movements)?

4. Do you ever cross-promote (e.g., Discord post linking to Facebook)? How?

5. What's your emoji strategy? Heavy? Sparse? Specific ones you gravitate to?

---

Format as a markdown document with:
- Platform-specific sections (Facebook, Discord, Primfeed, Teasers)
- 2-3 golden examples per platform
- Anti-examples (what sounds wrong)
- Structure templates (not rigid, but the typical flow)
- Tone notes per platform
- Emoji/formatting guide
- How the graphic pairs with the text
```

---

## Notes

- Interactive — needs your real posts or descriptions of what "right" sounds like.
- Check your Facebook post history, Discord messages, or Primfeed feed for actual examples you can paste in.
- Even rough/imperfect examples are useful — ChatGPT can identify patterns in what you consistently do.
