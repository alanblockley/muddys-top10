🎵 Spotify Integration

Connect your Spotify account to automatically generate weekly Top 10 playlists.

Checking connection status...

Manual Generation

Generate a playlist with the current Top 10 immediately, regardless of the scheduled time.

Automatic Generation Schedule

Configure when playlists should be automatically generated each week (PST timezone).

How it works:

  • Click "Connect Spotify Account" to authorize access
  • You'll be redirected to Spotify to approve permissions
  • Once connected, playlists will be created automatically based on your schedule
  • Playlists are public and named "Muddy's Top 10 - [Date]"
  • Each playlist contains up to 10 tracks from the current week's chart
  • You can also generate playlists manually at any time
  • The connection stays active unless you disconnect or change your Spotify password
